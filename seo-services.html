The Basics

What Is SEO?

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is the ongoing practice of improving a website's technical structure, content and authority so it ranks higher in unpaid search results — and gets surfaced inside AI-generated answers like Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity. It works by aligning a site with what search engines and AI models actually evaluate: whether the content directly and completely answers a real query, whether the technical foundation lets that content get crawled and understood, and whether outside signals — links, citations, reviews — back up the site's authority on the topic.

Done well, SEO compounds — each fixed page and published article keeps earning visibility long after a paid ad would have stopped running. That's what separates it from PPC, and why most marketing budgets treat it as the channel with the strongest long-term return.

Six Disciplines, Run as One SEO System

SEO services aren't one task — they're several disciplines that only work when they move together. Here's what's inside every Web Dominators SEO engagement.

Technical SEO

Site speed, crawlability, indexation, Core Web Vitals, structured data and mobile usability, fixed so search engines can actually read and trust your site.

On-Page SEO

Title tags, meta descriptions, header structure, internal linking and keyword mapping, aligned to what your buyers actually search for.

Off-Page SEO & Link Building

Digital PR, guest placements and citation building that earn authority signals instead of renting them from link farms.

Content Strategy & Creation

Topical maps and a publishing cadence built around real search intent, so content ranks and reads like it was written by someone who knows the subject.

Local SEO

Google Business Profile optimization, citation consistency and location pages for businesses that need to win map-pack and "near me" search.

AI Search Optimization (GEO/AEO)

Structuring content as clear, self-contained answers so it's eligible to be cited inside Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity, alongside traditional rankings.

Service Types

Which Type of SEO Service Does Your Business Need?

SEO services generally fall into five categories — local, national, e-commerce, technical and AI search optimization — and most businesses need a blend of two or three, not all five. Here's what each one actually covers.

Service Type Best For What's Included Typical Timeline
Local SEO Businesses with a physical location or service area Google Business Profile, citations, review strategy, location pages 60–90 days for first movement
National / Enterprise SEO Brands competing across the country or large sites (500+ pages) Site architecture, content at scale, technical audits, internal-linking systems 4–9 months
E-commerce SEO Online stores Product & category page optimization, schema, faceted-navigation fixes, commercial-intent content 3–6 months
Technical SEO Audit Sites with a traffic drop, migration or unexplained ranking loss Full crawl, Core Web Vitals, indexation and structured-data diagnosis 2–4 weeks for the audit itself
AI Search Optimization (GEO) Any business that wants to be cited by AI Overviews, ChatGPT or Perplexity Answer-island content structuring, schema, entity & E-E-A-T signals Runs alongside core SEO, not standalone
