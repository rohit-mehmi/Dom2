The Basics

What Is PPC?

PPC (Pay-Per-Click) is a digital advertising model where you pay a fee only when someone actually clicks your ad, rather than a flat fee for the ad simply being shown. Advertisers bid on keywords or audiences inside a platform like Google Ads, Microsoft Advertising, or Meta Ads; the platform then decides whose ad appears — and in what position — based on that bid combined with the ad's relevance and quality, not the highest bid alone.

PPC buys visibility, SEO earns it — that's the core difference, and it's why the two work best together rather than as a choice between them. PPC can put you at the top of results the same day a campaign launches; SEO compounds slower but keeps working long after you'd have had to stop paying for the click.
