The Basics

What Is Social Media Marketing?

Social media marketing is the practice of using platforms like Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook and X to build an audience, create demand, and turn attention into pipeline — through a mix of organic content, community management and paid social advertising. It works by showing up consistently with content native to each platform's format and culture, rather than posting the same asset everywhere and hoping it performs.

Organic social builds the audience and voice; paid social scales the content that's already working to new audiences. Most brands need both — organic alone is slow to compound, and paid alone burns budget without a foundation of trust to point to.

Strategy

The Content Mix

There's no universal formula, but the widely-used 80/20 rule is our starting point: the vast majority of what we post is built to earn attention and trust, with a small, deliberate slice reserved for direct promotion.

80% Value & Engagement 20% Promotion

Value & Engagement

Educational posts, industry insights, behind-the-scenes content, and direct engagement with your audience's own comments and posts.

Promotion

Product announcements, offers and direct calls-to-action — spaced out deliberately so the feed never feels like a sales pitch.
