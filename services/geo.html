Generative & Answer Engine Optimization (GEO) Services

Web Dominators' GEO services structure your content, entities and technical signals so generative engines — Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Copilot — can find, trust and cite you. Every engagement starts with a full AI-visibility audit, so the plan is built on where you're actually missing from AI answers today, not a generic checklist.

The Basics

What Is GEO?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) — also called AEO, or Answer Engine Optimization — is the practice of structuring a brand's content, data and entity signals so AI systems like Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Copilot can find, understand and cite it directly inside generated answers. It works by giving these systems what they actually parse: self-contained passages that fully answer one question, structured data that confirms who you are and what you offer, and consistent entity signals across the web that back up your authority on a topic.

GEO doesn't replace SEO — it runs on the same technical and content foundation, aimed at a different destination: not a ranked blue link, but the actual sentence an AI system chooses to quote. Most businesses need both, run together rather than as a choice between them.
